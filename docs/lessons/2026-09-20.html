Looking back on life choices (Conditional sentences, type 3)

If I had studied medicine instead of law, my life would have been completely different. My father always says that if he had accepted that job offer in Germany twenty years ago, our whole family would have moved there. I sometimes think that if I hadn't met my husband at that conference, we probably would never have gotten married. If the company had listened to our warnings, they wouldn't have lost so much money last year. My sister told me that if she had known how difficult the exam would be, she would have studied much harder. If we had left the house five minutes earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train. Looking back, I believe that if my parents hadn't encouraged me to keep practising the violin, I would have given up during my teenage years, and I would have regretted it deeply.

Traducción

Si hubiera estudiado medicina en lugar de derecho, mi vida habría sido completamente diferente. Mi padre siempre dice que si hubiera aceptado esa oferta de trabajo en Alemania hace veinte años, toda nuestra familia se habría mudado allí. A veces pienso que si no hubiera conocido a mi marido en aquella conferencia, probablemente nunca nos habríamos casado. Si la empresa hubiera escuchado nuestras advertencias, no habría perdido tanto dinero el año pasado. Mi hermana me dijo que si hubiera sabido lo difícil que sería el examen, habría estudiado mucho más. Si hubiéramos salido de casa cinco minutos antes, no habríamos perdido el tren. Mirando atrás, creo que si mis padres no me hubieran animado a seguir practicando el violín, habría abandonado durante mi adolescencia, y me habría arrepentido profundamente.

Notas de gramática

Conditional sentences type 3: 'si + pluperfect subjunctive + conditional perfect' for hypothetical past situations and their unrealised past consequences (si hubiera estudiado...habría sido; si hubiera aceptado...se habría mudado; si no hubiera conocido...habríamos casado).

Notas de vocabulario

'Mirando atrás' = looking back. 'Arrepentirse de' = to regret. 'Advertencias' = warnings.
